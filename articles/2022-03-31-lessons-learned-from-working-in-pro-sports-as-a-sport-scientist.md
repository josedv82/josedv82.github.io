<p>10 things I learned working in pro sport as sport scientist.</p>

<p>A visual thread<br>👇</p>

<p>You won’t have to sell anything if you are able to show links to performance.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/e001e42a-cba9-421d-98a8-4904b6c087ff/" alt="Illustration: You won’t have to sell anything if you are able to show links to performance." width="461" height="463" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Recovery is more about training than about resting.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/2d4514ca-fa46-49c3-a7b1-e67de9f63ed3/" alt="Illustration: Recovery is more about training than about resting." width="427" height="652" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Problem solve first. Innovation will come naturally.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/cbbd1199-878a-4693-b9e5-c251dea1d9e4/" alt="Illustration: Problem solve first. Innovation will come naturally." width="392" height="321" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>There is no shortage of questions. Learn the skills to answer them.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/427a20fc-9b36-4ed9-87e4-50112ccccfef/" alt="Illustration: There is no shortage of questions. Learn the skills to answer them." width="856" height="662" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Collect data only for the things you can’t see.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/3494b5b8-a646-4c74-a80d-4965330c9ed2/" alt="Illustration: Collect data only for the things you can’t see." width="457" height="471" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>The more complex is the environment the easier should be the solution.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/7f49efbe-e642-41e2-b3ef-2404cbfe6b40/" alt="Illustration: The more complex is the environment the easier should be the solution." width="527" height="497" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Health is a requirement. Not a goal.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/1d4ba737-43d0-4b18-b6ea-9dd7f779737e/" alt="Illustration: Health is a requirement. Not a goal." width="515" height="505" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Self-protection is detrimental in the long term. Hire the best and make adjustments if you have to.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/484b7d1d-fa5a-43cc-aa12-e34655f0dae5/" alt="Illustration: Self-protection is detrimental in the long term. Hire the best and make adjustments if you have to." width="682" height="296" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Advantages are temporary. Continuously audit your processes to keep finding them.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/5750e6cd-d428-4968-81dd-4a5c13b5d24a/" alt="Illustration: Advantages are temporary. Continuously audit your processes to keep finding them." width="680" height="470" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>Work-life balance is hard. Those who chase it end up being frustrated at both ends. </p>

<p>Get in, sprint (for as long as you want) then get out.</p>

<p>Ensure those around you are onboard.</p>

<figure class="lesson-figure"><img src="https://api.typefully.com/media-p/1a35e841-5e95-4940-859b-fca08b119cdc/" alt="Illustration: Work-life balance is hard. Those who chase it end up being frustrated at both ends. " width="612" height="523" loading="lazy" decoding="async"></figure>

<p>I am sure some of these are controversial.</p>

<p>I’d love to hear from others with experience in the field.</p>
